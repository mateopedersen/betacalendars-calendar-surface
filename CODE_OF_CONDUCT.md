# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free project for
everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and constructive.
- Explain disagreement without personal attacks.
- Respect differing perspectives and accessibility needs.
- Focus feedback on the work and its impact.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, and sharing another
person's private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate this
code and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. To report an
issue, contact the project maintainers privately through the repository's
security contact.
